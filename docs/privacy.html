Chef Next Door — Privacy Policy

Last updated [DATE]

Chef Next Door shows you which home cooks near you are cooking right now, and lets you message them. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Chef Next Door iOS app and the service behind it.

What we collect, and why

All of it is to make the app work. None of it is sold, and none of it is used for advertising or tracking.

Your address, if you cook

The map never shows where you live. Your kitchen is drawn as an approximate area, not a point on your street, and your exact address is released only when you choose to share it inside a conversation. This is deliberate and it is enforced by the service, not only by the app.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. The app contains none.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No tracking you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication, file storage and notification delivery.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and push notifications. Apple’s private relay means we may never see your real email address.
  • Los Angeles County Department of Public Health — we read their published MEHKO register to check permit numbers. We send them nothing.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Account → Delete my account, or from the menu in the top right of your kitchen if you cook. It is immediate and permanent. It removes your account, your kitchen and menu, your photos, your conversations and the cooks you follow.

Two things survive, and we would rather say so than let you discover it. A safety report you filed about someone else is kept, with your identity removed from it — otherwise a report could be erased by the person who filed it. And messages you sent remain visible in the other person’s copy of the conversation, in the same way an email you sent stays in the recipient’s inbox.

How long we keep things

Your content stays until you delete it or delete your account. Safety reports are kept while they are useful for protecting people, in the anonymised form described above.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your information. Deletion is available directly in the app; for anything else, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in California, this includes your rights under the CCPA — and note that we do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Chef Next Door is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
